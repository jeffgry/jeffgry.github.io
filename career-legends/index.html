Career Legends

A 1v1 profession card battler for iPhone. Build a deck of careers, battle bots or other players, and climb the leagues.

Frequently asked

  • Do I need an account? No. Practice, Career Journey, the Shop and deck building work without one. Online play uses Sign in with Apple.
  • How do I delete my account? In the game: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Are there in-app purchases? No. Card packs use coins earned in the game or from optional rewarded ads.
  • Someone has an offensive name. Tap the flag icon next to their name on the leaderboard or after an online match.
