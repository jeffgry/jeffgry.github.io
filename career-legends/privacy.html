Career Legends — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026
Developer / data controller: Oğuzhan Akdoğan, Türkiye
Contact: oguzakdogaan@gmail.com

What this policy covers

Career Legends ("the game") is a card game for iPhone. You can play Practice matches, the Career Journey and use the Shop without an account. An account is only needed for online play.

Data we process

Without an account (stored only on your phone)

Your practice profile, coins, cards, decks, achievements, journey progress and settings are saved on your phone. We do not receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

With an account (Sign in with Apple)

  • Apple sign-in: a user identifier from Apple and the email address Apple shares (this can be an Apple "Hide My Email" relay address). We do not receive your Apple password.
  • Your display name (chosen by you, visible to other players on the leaderboard and in matches).
  • Game data: level, XP, online coins, rating and league, wins and losses, unlocked online cards, online decks, pack results, online matches (moves, results, timestamps) and friend invite codes.
  • Reports you send about another player's name, and reports others send about yours.

Advertising (rewarded ads, optional)

Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one for a reward. They are served by Google AdMob, which may collect device and usage information (for example IP address, device model, ad interactions) under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). In the EEA, UK and where required, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. The game does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking.

Why we use it

To run your account and online matches, show leaderboards, keep the game fair (reward limits, preventing abuse), act on name reports, and show the rewarded ads you choose to watch.

Legal bases (GDPR / KVKK): performance of our agreement with you (account and online play), legitimate interests (fair play, security, moderation) and your consent (personalised ads where required).

Who processes it

  • Supabase (database, authentication and server functions) stores the account data above. Region: Northeast Asia (Tokyo, Japan).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple.
  • Google: AdMob rewarded ads and the consent form.

Because our servers are in Japan and our service providers operate internationally, account data is transferred outside Türkiye and the EU. We rely on the safeguards these providers offer for such transfers. We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. When you delete it, your profile, decks, cards, coins, rating, reports and invites are deleted immediately; past online matches stay for your opponents' history with your name replaced by "Former player". Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.

Your rights

You can see and change your display name in the game, delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account, and contact us at oguzakdogaan@gmail.com to request access to, correction of, or a copy of your data. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or Türkiye under KVKK) you may also have the right to object, restrict processing and complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We will post changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date.
